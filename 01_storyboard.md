# Main storyboard and presenter cues

For a 30-minute panel, this is a presenter-paced segment, not an auto-advancing slide deck. The audience sees eight **beats** and 26 meaningful advances. Beat 7's acts and the final Ghost reveals add depth without adding a ninth beat. Each answer creates the next question; the story never backtracks.

## Trace continuity

The failed trade is `EVT-0001` in the 486-trace-event synthetic run. `EntityGraph/T-17` (`EVT-0238`) remains unhighlighted during the flood. It changes a derived Entity X classification; the linked Investigator event moves risk **39 → 76**, the Interpreter proposes restriction, and PolicyMapper identifies the mandate boundary. The Supervisory AI links those original event IDs. The original central-bank-related source supports a **76 → 39** counterfactual and is never overwritten. The Bank Compliance challenge is appended separately. The runtime gate evaluates the original proposal with disputed evidence; it does not execute a restriction.

## Authority and trust

Opening ladder language: **ASSIST** retrieves, summarises and organises; **INVESTIGATE** chooses tools, evidence and queries; **ASSESS** forms risk views, prioritises and recommends; **ACT** executes, restricts, transfers or intervenes. **SUPERVISE** arrives later as a meta-control layer, not an extra rung. These meanings are presenter notes, not four more stage cards.

The machine activity loop may contain hundreds of queries, retrievals and tool actions. A separate simulated supervisory control reduces those to a material issue. The AI investigator then recommends intervention from its 76 / 74% assessment, before the no-breach boundary is revealed. A Bank Compliance Agent later submits counter-evidence; that challenge creates a contested fact, not a replacement truth. A human judges the source fact and intervention. This makes the supervisory control an **attention allocator**; the main stage asks about provenance, uncertainty and contestability, and the drawer exposes the full eight-control framework. The control may flag and escalate, never silently intervene.

## Beat 7 presenter rhythm

The six portfolios have different objectives and constraints; they do not communicate. **MARKET SIGNAL** introduces volatility up, market depth down and expected downside up. Their staggered, individually rational decisions converge visually into the cyan **DE-RISK / SELL** action. Pause on **6 / 6 WITHIN MANDATE ✓** and **NO COORDINATION REQUIRED.** The original signal remains faint background history, not competing copy.

**EXECUTE** begins with **THE MARKET CHANGES.** Staggered first-wave flows reduce depth while spread and volatility rise. The same cyan action path then returns to the agents as **CHANGED MARKET → AGENTS RE-OPTIMISE**; each responds in sequence before a stronger second wave follows. The mandate line disappears once its point has landed. Pause on **CORRELATION BECOMES FEEDBACK** while previous-state detail recedes. **FOLLOW THE CONSEQUENCES** clears the portfolio cards: one changed market state branches into funding/leverage, collateral and settlement/cash channels. All three remain readable together; the next presenter advance converges them on **SYSTEM LIQUIDITY PRESSURE ↑**. This is an illustrative possibility, not a strict serial dependency or inevitable outcome. Clear the diagram, then pause separately on **NO AGENT FAILED.** and **THE SYSTEM CHANGED.** The bridge asks **WHO IS SUPERVISING THE SYSTEM?** before the Ghost stage clears the room.

Final spoken synthesis: “Autonomous AI may begin as a productivity engine, become an operational actor, and increasingly participate in supervision. The question is not simply whether the future supervisor is human or AI. It is how we design a financial system in which machines can act at machine speed, while judgement, authority and accountability remain deliberately governed.” Then reveal **WHERE DOES INTELLIGENCE END AND AUTHORITY BEGIN?** and stop.
