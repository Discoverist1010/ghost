## Authority and trust

Opening ladder language: **ASSIST** retrieves, summarises and organises; **INVESTIGATE** chooses tools, evidence and queries; **ASSESS** forms risk views, prioritises and recommends; **ACT** executes, restricts, transfers or intervenes. **SUPERVISE** arrives later as a meta-control layer, not an extra rung. These meanings are presenter notes, not four more stage cards.

The machine activity loop may contain hundreds of queries, retrievals and tool actions. A separate simulated supervisory control reduces those to a material issue. The AI investigator then recommends intervention from its 76 / 74% assessment, before the no-breach boundary is revealed. A Bank Compliance Agent later submits counter-evidence; that challenge prevents the first AI from settling the matter alone but is not replacement truth. The conditional counterfactual demonstrates sensitivity, not a final evidence verdict. The runtime gate stops autonomous execution while that dispute remains unresolved, then hands confidence and validation to human judgement.
