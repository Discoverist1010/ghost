# Presentation UI and Wireframes

Design for 1920×1080 projection in a large conference room. The screen should feel like a high-end institutional command centre and decision theatre. At each major moment the audience should identify the one change in under two seconds: **What changed? Why does it matter? Who has authority now?** The earlier learn/want/authorised questions remain a design test, never a permanent footer.

## Type, contrast and space

- Hero numbers and state changes: approximately 110–170px.
- Hero verdicts: approximately 64–90px.
- Major headlines: approximately 54–76px.
- Primary conclusions: approximately 34–48px.
- Supporting text that must be read: at least approximately 26–30px.
- Presenter button text: approximately 22–26px or larger, with large targets and clear focus states.
- The deliberately unreadable audit stream is the sole exception; the material event's consequence appears later at full scale, while the event ID remains in Evidence.

Use a deep navy / near-black field, near-white type, restrained cyan for observation, amber for authority/escalation and red only for genuine denial or danger. Pair color with words; color alone does not carry the meaning. Presentation mode has no vertical scroll at 1920×1080. The small top-left identity is **AUTONOMOUS AI // FUTURE SUPERVISORS**. A quiet top-right narrative indicator moves from AI role / human benefit to AI role / governance question across the eight beats. A small corner control shows only Back, the current beat out of eight, and Next. Contextual detail controls say **SHOW MORE**; technical headings stay in the drawer.

## Eight audience beats

1. **From answers to actions:** autonomous finance moves from answers to actions; operations, transactions and portfolios are signposts. ASSIST → INVESTIGATE → ASSESS → ACT leads to “AUTONOMOUS TO DO WHAT?” SUPERVISE is a later meta-control, not a rung in this ladder.
2. **Agents help — humans lose the thread:** “1 FAILED TRADE” branches into specialist workstreams and “8 AGENTS / 27 SUB-TASKS.” Reassuring oversight claims meet the actual 486-trace-event flood in 3.2 seconds. AUTHORISE / REJECT reveals “ON WHAT BASIS?” without recording approval.
3. **AI supervises AI:** “486 TRACE EVENTS → 1 MATERIAL ISSUE” compresses the flood. “WHO DECIDED WHAT THE HUMAN SAW?” exposes the reliance problem. Entity X remains hidden; provenance, uncertainty and contestability are subdued or in Evidence.
4. **Prediction is not permission:** “AI INVESTIGATOR ASSESSMENT” introduces 76 risk and 74% illustrative prediction, followed by an explicit, underlined **AI RECOMMENDS / ENHANCED LIQUIDITY RESTRICTION**. The next state clears to “NO RULE HAS BEEN BREACHED / PREDICTION ≠ PERMISSION.”
5. **One fact changes the case:** a single stage has three presenter states. “WHY 76?” animates classification → risk → recommendation within one click; “CHECK SOURCE” contrasts the derived commercial classification with the central-bank-related source, then names the Bank Compliance Agent and its counter-evidence. **FACT CONTESTED** resolves to “THE SECOND AI DOES NOT SETTLE THE MATTER. IT PREVENTS THE FIRST AI FROM SETTLING IT ALONE.” The challenge does not become a second AI verdict. “RUN COUNTERFACTUAL” visibly collapses 76 → 39 while the recommendation retracts.
6. **The right loop / authority:** the first state shows only **EVIDENCE: DISPUTED**, **MANDATE: RECOMMEND ONLY**, huge amber **ESCALATE**, and **RESTRICTION NOT EXECUTED**. Its punch is “THE SYSTEM DOESN'T NEED TO KNOW WHO IS RIGHT IN ORDER TO KNOW IT SHOULD NOT ACT.” The next state is **HUMAN JUDGEMENT REQUIRED**, with manual verification, AI-assisted validation and **WHO VALIDATES THE VALIDATOR?** Its final line is “AUTHORITY MAY BE HUMAN. CERTAINTY MAY STILL DEPEND ON MACHINES.” Identity, materiality, evidence, execution authority, gate rule and reason are in Show More; there is no main-stage runtime checklist.
7. **When correct agents become a system:** one persistent stage opens on **MANY AGENTS. DIFFERENT GOALS. SIMILAR DECISIONS.** Six compact portfolio nodes surround a dominant common market signal. **OPTIMISE:** agent focus moves A→F; cyan decisions and inward arrows converge on the two-line cyan hero **DE-RISK / SELL**. The prior signal drops to faint continuity. One white **6 / 6 WITHIN MANDATE** status with a cyan tick replaces six competing ticks, followed by “NO COORDINATION REQUIRED.” **FEEDBACK:** the mandate status disappears; the eye starts at **THE MARKET CHANGES**, follows aggregate depth/spread/volatility, then follows the same cyan thread back as **CHANGED MARKET → AGENTS RE-OPTIMISE** and through staggered second-wave actions. Cards and market detail recede for “CORRELATION BECOMES FEEDBACK.” **TRANSMIT:** clear portfolio detail. A small **6 AGENTS → DE-RISK / SELL** callback sits above **MARKET CONDITIONS CHANGE**. Cyan connectors branch to three parallel, persistent channels—funding/leverage, collateral, settlement/cash. The next click visibly joins those channels into amber **SYSTEM LIQUIDITY PRESSURE ↑**. Clear the diagram for “NO AGENT FAILED.” and then “THE SYSTEM CHANGED.” A short bridge asks “WHO IS SUPERVISING THE SYSTEM?” The research/synthetic qualification remains in documentation and detail, not on the main stage.
8. **The ghost:** clear the stage. Progressively reveal “WHO IS THE SUPERVISOR?”, “WHERE IS THE RIGHT LOOP?”, “WHO SUPERVISES THE SUPERVISORY AI?”, then the three propositions about distributed intelligence, deliberate authority and accountability. End on **WHERE DOES INTELLIGENCE END AND AUTHORITY BEGIN?** No extra slide or checklist.

## Secondary views and controls

Evidence drawers are optional drill-down views for stable `EVT-XXXX` event IDs, `AGT-…` demo-agent identities, timestamps, mandates, evidence, fingerprints, causal links, human review and the full eight Supervisory AI trust controls. The audience never needs to open one to understand the story. Source maturity and synthetic qualifications stay in the README, public-source notes and drawer detail—not in main-stage chrome.

Keyboard: Right/Space next, Left previous, R replay the current animation, P pause/resume it, F fullscreen, Esc close detail, Home reset. Motion represents a state change—branching, the real log burst, 486 → 1, causal reconstruction, gate checks or 76 → 39. No autonomous transition may force the presenter past a discussion pause.
